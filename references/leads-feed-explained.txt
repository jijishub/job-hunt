Lead Feed Behavior

The 10 preset opportunities have been removed completely. Their roles, salaries, source labels and match scores were manually coded suggestions, not verified vacancies or submitted applications. No admin or member receives this preset feed, and there is no scheduled job that recreates it.

Cleanup: two saved leads originating from that feed were removed from the live database. One linked Notion entry was archived to prevent sync from restoring it. The remaining two saved leads and all 49 applications were preserved.

My Saved Leads now contains only leads saved to your account. Quick Add stores a separate lead record; it does not submit a job application. Promotion moves a lead into the application tracker. Existing company suggestions remain available without creating new companies for leads.

Direct Sourcing Launchpads open external job-search pages using your search terms. They do not import or save listings automatically.

AI Search remains a separate, user-triggered tool. It requests six suggestions per call from the configured AI API. These suggestions and search links are not independently verified vacancies; check the original posting before saving or applying. There is no automatic search schedule.

Technical reference: src/app/api/leads/route.ts returns an empty discovery list for authenticated clients. The Leads view no longer requests or renders the preset feed.
